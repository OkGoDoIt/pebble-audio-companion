Privacy
This app has no account of its own. Nothing you record, transcribe, or write is sent to its developer, and this app uses none of it for training, advertising, or profiling. Your server holds a copy of the text because you linked it.
Your recordings and transcripts are never sold. They go only where this screen says they go.
The official Pebble app never receives your audio.
Diagnostic logs stay on this phone. They hold no audio and no transcript text, and are only ever shared if you send them yourself from Diagnostics.
Recordings, transcripts, and notes are stored in this app on your iPhone, and are included in the iPhone's usual backups.
Spotlight's index of your recordings is kept on this iPhone by iOS. What goes into it — nothing, titles and summaries, or everything — is your choice on this screen.
