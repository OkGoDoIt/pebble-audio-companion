AI Assistants, as a native Settings flow

Assistant access is an optional iOS setting, not a feature to sell. It lives as one row, AI Assistants · Off, beside Privacy. Setup is a modal sheet in the spirit of Health’s Sharing: an introduction that says what goes (text) and what stays (audio), then one step per screen. Link your server by camera or code. Choose what assistants may do, with clips and follow-ups off and the history scope explicit. Connect each assistant inside that assistant and watch its name appear here. The linked page answers, in order: is it working, who reads, what they may do, and the way out. Privacy keeps its stance card and gains one row, AI assistants can read, that names the readers.

  • The row is named for the goal, not the machinery. “AI Assistants” replaces “Pebble Audio server” on the Settings root; the server appears only where the person must act on it (linking, the status line).
  • Nothing syncs until the choices step is confirmed. Cancel at any step leaves the phone as it was; that is why the sheet keeps Cancel after linking.
  • One added choice: which conversations. “All conversations” (with the count the phone already knows) or “From today on”. Consent to history deserves its own answer.
  • Connection happens in the assistant. The phone shows each assistant’s own path, offers Open in Claude and a Copy for the address, and turns a row into a green Connected as soon as the server reports it. No codes or keys are shown on the phone; the Mac tools get theirs on the server.
  • Privacy names readers, by position. The stance headline says who can read (“ChatGPT and Claude can read your text”), and the new row repeats it as a value you can tap. The deletion note says plainly that what an assistant already read is up to that assistant.
  • No new motif. Stance card, grouped lists, OptionCard, switches, and tiles are the app’s own; every color is an existing token.
  • Contrast fix. Footnotes and row values use Tokens.tertiary (#6E6E73, 4.6:1 on the ground) instead of Tokens.meta (#8A8A8E, 3.1:1), which fails AA at 13 pt.
All content is invented. Phone screens are 390 × 844 pt, drawn from ios/App/DesignSystem/Tokens.swift and the Settings components. Open any screen on its own for the full-size page; PNGs are in shots/, the contact sheet is sheet.png.
