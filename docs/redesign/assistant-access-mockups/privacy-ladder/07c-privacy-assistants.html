Privacy

Nothing you record, transcribe, or write is sent to this app’s developer, and none of it is used for training, advertising, or profiling. Your server holds a copy of the text because you linked it.
Deleting a conversation here deletes it on your server. What an assistant already read stays in your chats with it.
Your recordings and transcripts are never sold or shared. They go only where this screen says they go.
