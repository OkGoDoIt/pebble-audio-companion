Fewer questions

Same Thursday, October 22. Four follow-ups were clear (three you asked for out loud and one you marked on your watch), so they’re already reminders. Three were only heard, and those are the cards.

What changed from v3
  • What you asked for out loud, or marked on your watch, becomes a reminder by itself and never reaches the cards.
  • The cards are only for the unsure ones. One gesture, one meaning: right or ✓ is Remind me, left or ✕ is Skip. No checkbox, dots, or count button.
  • After the last card, one list shows everything from today and what happened to each. Tap any row to change it; that list is the only undo.
  • With nothing unsure there are no cards, just “4 reminders added from today,” which opens the same list.
  • Switching where reminders are kept moves all of them. There is always one home.
