Pebble Audio · Follow-ups to Reminders · v2

One review, already filled in

Same Thursday, October 22, same seven new follow-ups. The ones you asked for out loud, the one you marked on your watch, and the one due today start ticked; the other two don’t.

What changed from v1
  • “You asked for” is gone everywhere. How sure we are only decides what starts ticked, as in D.
  • The review is A’s card stack, but every card arrives with its choice made, and a row of dots shows all seven choices at once.
  • One button the whole time, “Add 5 to Reminders,” counting as you change cards. Tap it straight away, or swipe through first; skipped cards keep their pre-set choice.
  • Nothing goes to Reminders on its own. Today, the Follow-ups tab, and an optional evening notification all open the same review.
1

Finding it, and the review

The fast path is open, glance at the dots, tap the button. The careful path is swiping: right adds, left keeps it in Follow-ups (nothing is thrown away), and tapping the choice on a card flips it. A swipe sets the choice and moves on.

Afterwards, and Settings

What went in carries the list’s color dot and “In Reminders,” nothing more. Ticking it off in Reminders ticks it here. Settings is one short screen: where they go, alerts, and the evening review.
