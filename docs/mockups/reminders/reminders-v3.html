Pebble Audio · Reminders · v3

Follow-ups become reminders

Same Thursday, October 22, same seven new follow-ups. Five start as “Remind me”: the three you asked for, the one marked on your watch, and the one due today.

What changed from v2
  • Two words only. A follow-up is something the app noticed; a reminder is one you said yes to.
  • The tab is now Reminders. Follow-ups wait at the top of it, and on Today, until you review them.
  • Where reminders live is one setting: Pebble Audio (the default) or Apple Reminders. The list looks and works the same either way.
  • The review keeps v2’s cards, but the choice is “Remind me” or “Skip.” Skipped ones leave the queue and stay findable in their conversation and in search.
  • Reminders you ask Muse, Siri, or ChatGPT to set skip the review, with one quiet line saying where they came from.
1

Reviewing follow-ups

Today and an optional evening notification lead to the same review. Each card arrives already decided; the dots show all seven at a glance. Tap the button straight away, or swipe first: right is Remind me, left is Skip.

2

The Reminders tab, in either home

The same screen whether reminders are kept in Pebble Audio or in Apple Reminders. In the second case the list is the Apple Reminders list, read back, so a tick in either app is the same tick. In-app reminders alert like real ones.

3

One setting decides the home

Settings › Reminders. Apple Reminders is opt-in. iOS asks for access only when you pick it, and then the app offers to move what you already have, once.
